How it works

Three steps. No editing on your end.

You don't need to trim anything. Raw, shaky, zoomed-in phone video from the stands is fine.

Send your clips

Fill out the request form and share a Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud link with your game footage. Phone videos, Hudl screen recordings and TV clips all work.

We build the reel

We find the snap and the end of every play, cut the plays together and sync them to your song. Premium reels also get the player spotlight and an animated intro.

Get your link

You get an HD download and a YouTube link you can send to coaches, post on social or add to your Hudl and recruiting profiles.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

What footage can I send?

Anything you have. Phone video from the stands (even zoomed-in and shaky), Hudl screen recordings, TV clips and photos all work. Send the whole game clips; we find the plays.

How do I get the clips to you?

Upload them to a Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud folder and paste the share link in the form. Make sure the link is set so anyone with it can view.

How many plays should I send?

Most reels have 10 to 25 plays. Send everything you think is worth including; if you have favorites, tell us in the notes and we'll make sure they're in.

How do you know which player is mine?

Tell us the jersey number and color in the form. For Premium, we mark your player by hand in every play so the spotlight stays on the right kid.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Every reel includes one round of changes, like reordering plays, removing a play or swapping the song.

Do you do other sports?

Football is what we're built for, but ask in the notes. Other sports work as long as there's clear footage of your athlete.
