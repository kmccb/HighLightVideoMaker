How to use Highlight Reels

From a folder of clips to a finished reel. The fast path is: New project → Fix-ups → Build reel. The review page is there for fixing the snap and end of a play.

1Start the app

  • Double-click Highlight Reels on the desktop. It opens in your browser. If it is already running it just opens the page.
  • The app runs in a small minimised window called Highlight Reels. Closing that window stops the app.
  • The start screen lists your projects (one per player). Click one to open it. The bar above the list filters (All / In progress / Reel built), sorts, and switches between List and Grid.

2New project

  1. On the start screen click + New project.
  2. Pick the player (or quick-add a new one).
  3. Browse to the folder with that player's clips (photos in the same folder are picked up too).
  4. Choose the song: a music file, the player's last song, or paste a YouTube link.
  5. The app makes preview copies and finds the snap and end of every play. This takes a few minutes; you can watch the progress.
Clips from Google Drive: they are on your G: drive (Google Drive for desktop), so you can browse straight to G:\My Drive.

3Getting around

  • The rail on the left is on every page: Projects, Review, Fix-ups (with a count of plays that still need you), Build, Publish, Players, and at the bottom Activity, search and this Guide.
  • The stage bar at the top shows where the reel is: Review → Fix-ups → Build → Publish. Click a stage to go there.
  • Ctrl K opens a search box: type a page, a play or a project and press Enter.
  • Activity (the round arrow near the bottom of the rail) shows what the app is doing right now (setting up a project, building a reel) and its log. If something looks stuck, open it: "last log line 5 s ago" means it is still working. Copy log copies it so you can send it on.
  • The small SD / HD / FHD / QHD / 4K badge on a play is the quality of the original video. Hover over it for the exact size.

4Check each play (review page)

The app guesses where each play starts (the snap, green) and ends (red). The snap is usually close; the end is worth a quick look.

The page has three parts: the plays on the left, the video and timeline in the middle, and the settings for this play on the right (Trim, Your player, Big moment, Notes), with Reset and Confirm & next at the bottom.

  • Bigger video: click the « at the top of the plays list to fold it down to a strip of thumbnails. Click it again to bring the full list back. An amber outline on a thumbnail means that play still needs you. The app remembers your choice.
  • All / Needs you / In reel / Left out above the list show only those plays. "Needs you" = in the reel but not confirmed, not clicked at the snap, or without a hit. The box above them filters by game, file name or note.
  • Each row shows a frame of the play, how long it runs in the reel, and what's set: spotlight, HIT, or what is missing.
  • The In reel switch above the video (or X) leaves a play out or puts it back.
  • The keyboard button above the video (or ?) shows every shortcut.

5Fix-ups: the player and the hit

Open Fix-ups from the stage bar at the top (or the warning icon in the left rail); the badge shows how many plays still need you. One card per play; "only plays that still need it" hides the ones already done.

Each card opens at the snap. Click your player (a magnifier follows the mouse). If he is hidden at the snap, step a little and click him there. Tracking him through the play starts by itself.

7Photos

  • Photos in the project folder (JPG, PNG, HEIC) are found automatically, also ones you add later.
  • They appear under the plays on the review page and in the reel order on the Build screen. Untick one to leave it out; click it to preview the slide.
  • They're placed by the date they were taken, next to the plays from the same day. Drag them anywhere in the reel order.
  • Each photo is a slow pan-and-zoom slide over a blurred fill; Photo length (3/4/5 s) is fitted to the beat.
Phone screenshots look like a small picture inside a frame. You'll usually want to untick those.

8Build the reel

Click Continue to Build at the top right, or Build in the rail or the stage bar. The Build screen uses the whole window: the reel on the left, the settings on the right, and the Build reel button pinned at the bottom right. Esc goes back to review.

The reel (left side)

  • Storyboard: one tile per play or photo, as wide as it lasts. A yellow bar under a tile = the hit is set; an amber outline = it needs a fix (no freeze, not confirmed, or the same footage twice). Click a tile to jump to it in the list below.
  • Preview with the song and the Music lane: see section 9.
  • Reel order (the list): drag to reorder, untick to leave out, Preview renders one play. ☆ stars a play for the recruiting cut, ⚡ shows which plays are big plays (section 10). Tags warn you about plays with no freeze or an unconfirmed trim.
  • If the same footage is in the reel twice (for example a clip and a Hudl recording of it), a warning at the top names them and offers Leave out.

The settings (right side)

Five groups; click a heading to open or close it. The line under each heading says what is set, so you can check everything without opening them.

  • Output. Make: Highlight reel, Recruiting cut or Both (section 12). Format: Wide (16:9, TV/YouTube/Hudl), Vertical (9:16 for Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat: each play is re-cut from the original, following your player) or Both (two files; about twice as long to build). Player: whose name and colours to use.
  • Story.
    • Intro: click Change ▸ to see them all (Slam, Inferno, Thunder, Shatter, Stadium, Glitch, Smoke, Neon, Warp, Gold, Classic). Hover a tile to watch it, click to choose it and hear it. ▶ With my player renders it with this player's name, colours and song.
    • The Journey: an opening that goes from him as a little kid to now (section 11).
    • Intro timing: Steady beat, or Big hits (the number, name and logo slam in on the song's heaviest moments).
    • Into first play: Crossfade, or the intro blows apart into Bricks or Smoke on a big hit of the song.
    • Transition between plays (crossfade / cut / dip to black), Name banner (first play / every play / off), Freeze intro (freeze and spotlight on him at the snap), Ring with spotlight, Photo length.
    • Match cuts (off unless you tick it): a play ends with your player on one spot of the screen and the next play opens with him on the same spot at the same size, so the cut feels like one shot. It only happens where he is marked near the end of one play and at the start of the next, so click him at the snap and near the end of plays to get more of them. More about match cuts ▾
  • Music. My song (a file or a YouTube link; hits are synced to its beats) or Made for this reel: the app writes a Rock, Trap/rap or Cinematic track around your plays, with an impact on every hit; ▶ Hear style plays a demo and New take gives a different key and chords. No copyright problems on Instagram/TikTok. Also Game audio level and Sync hits to beat.
  • Motion. Slow-mo into hit (0.5× / 0.4× / 0.3×), Hit punch (a quick zoom bump and flash), and the big-play effects (section 10).
  • Ending. Ending style: Forge, Anvil strike, Molten, Minimal, or Hammer and Quench, which have their own sound (one choice for all your reels). ScottForge card turns the animated ending on or off.

Press Build reel. The screen switches to Renders & publish and shows the progress. Plays already rendered are reused, so rebuilding after small changes is quicker.

Match cuts in detail

Normally when one play ends and the next begins, your player jumps to wherever he happens to be in the new shot, and the eye has to find him again. A match cut removes that jump: he is on the same spot of the screen, at the same size, on both sides of the cut.

What you see:

  1. Over the last moments of the first play (under half a second) the picture pushes in and slides until he sits on a chosen spot.
  2. The picture holds him there through the transition.
  3. The next play opens already pushed in, with him on that same spot at the same size.
  4. That play then eases back out to its normal framing.

With the Crossfade transition he appears to dissolve into himself. The app picks the spot nearest the middle of the screen that both plays can reach, and zooms the play where he is smaller a little more so his height matches. Nothing is re-timed: plays keep their length and hits still land on the same beats.

A cut stays a normal cut when:

  • he isn't being followed near the end of the first play (the app looks up to 1 second back from the end);
  • he isn't being followed at the start of the next play (it looks from the start up to the snap);
  • there is a photo between the two plays;
  • the play is too short to move the picture in.

To get more of them: click him at the snap, and click him once near the end of the play.

Match cuts are off until you tick them, because they touch every cut between plays, not just the big ones. On plays that already zoom and follow him he is near the middle anyway, so the match is mostly about size and looks subtle. They aren't shown in "Preview with the song"; you see them in the built reel. After a build, Activity shows how many cuts were matched ("match cuts: 3").

9Preview with the song, and the music lane

Preview with the song

Under the storyboard. It plays the plays and the song together, timed exactly as the build will time them, so you can see where things land before you wait for a build. Change the song or any setting and it updates.

  • ▶ Play preview plays it. Tags on the picture say FREEZE, SLOW-MO and HIT; the picture flashes yellow on each hit and the small light blinks on every beat.
  • The lane under the player is the whole reel: plays, freezes, photos, the song with its fades, and ticks for the beats. A yellow hit marker is on the beat, a red one is off. Click or drag to move through the reel.
  • Zoom in shows 20 seconds around the playhead, with every beat.
  • The list on the right says where each hit lands ("on the beat", "0.21 s late"). Click a row to play from just before that hit.
The preview shows timing, not the finished look: no zoom or ring, no transitions, no game sound, and it is always wide. The intro, photos and ending are placeholder cards, and a big play's freeze shows a held frame.

10Big plays

For his one or two best plays. You make a play a big play in the same place you mark its hit:

  • Review page: on the right under Big moment, below the Hit button, set ⚡ Big play to one of the six kinds below (Off makes it a normal play again).
  • Fix-ups → Hit tab: the ⚡ Big play list under each play's filmstrip. A play that already has its hit is hidden there until you untick only plays that still need it.

The play needs a hit, and he needs to be clicked on it; the review page tells you if one is missing. In the Build screen's reel order, big plays show their kind, for example ⚡ TRAIL or ⚡ REPLAY.

For a run (a long gain, a return, a touchdown)

  • Trail: on the hit the picture freezes for about 4 seconds, pulls back, and copies of your player appear along the path he ran, popping in on the beat.
  • 3D: on the hit the picture freezes and the camera swings round him, with his name. If a trail can't be made (he hardly moved), the app makes a 3D freeze instead.

For a hit (a tackle, a block, a collision)

  • Shock (shockwave): a ripple spreads out from the contact and bends the picture, with a hard shake. The play doesn't stop.
  • Impact (impact frames): for a quarter of a second the picture snaps to stark black and white with speed lines, like a comic-book hit, then carries on.
  • Triple (triple hit): the hit plays three times on three beats of the song, closer and louder each time. Adds about a second.
  • Replay (instant replay): just after the hit the tape runs back and the hit plays again in slow motion, pushed in on him, with a REPLAY tag. Adds about three and a half seconds.

Triple and Replay hold the reel up by a whole number of beats, so the plays after them still land on the beat. In "Preview with the song" they show as a block on the lane; Shock and Impact don't change the timing, so there is nothing to see there until you build.

What a big play gets is set under Motion on the Build screen (all on to begin with; nothing happens until a play has ⚡):

  • Effect on the hit: the kind you picked for the play, from the six above.
  • Only him in colour: the second and a half before the hit drains to black and white, except your player. Colour comes back on the hit.
  • Silence, then the drop: all sound cuts out for a moment before the hit, then a boom lands on it.
  • Song jumps to a big moment: after that silence the song comes back on one of its biggest moments (a chorus or a heavy hit) instead of just carrying on. Needs the drop, beat sync, and your own song.
Big plays add to the build: a trail about 15 seconds, a 3D freeze over a minute, the four hit effects a few seconds each, "only him in colour" about half a minute each. Vertical reels get their own tall version. Big-play effects are left out of the recruiting cut.
Keep it to one or two plays per reel. If every play is a big play, none of them is.

11The Journey

An opening that shows him as a little kid, then cuts through the years to now, and lands on a heavy hit of the song. It takes the place of the intro. Turn it on under Story → The Journey and type the year he was Born (for the age shown with each year).

The old shots are found by themselves. For that to work:

  1. Make a project from each old season's folder (+ New project), and pick the same player for it.
  2. In each one, click him in a play or two (Fix-ups → Player). Marking a hit picks the exact moment shown; otherwise the app uses the middle of where you clicked him.

The line next to Born says what was found, for example "Old plays found: 2016 (3)". The "now" shots are this project's best clicked plays with a hit (⚡ and ☆ plays first).

If the old footage can't be reached when you build (for example the network drive is off), the reel gets the normal intro instead.

12Recruiting cut and a copy per coach

  • Under Output → Make choose Recruiting cut (or Both). It is made for college coaches: an info card at the start and end (name, number, position, class, size, school, GPA, Hudl link), then the plays with the freeze on him. No photos, no name banner, no big-play effects.
  • Click the ☆ next to his best 3-5 plays in the reel order. Starred plays open the cut, then the reel's other plays follow until it's about 3 minutes.
  • Add GPA and the Hudl link on Players.
  • A copy per coach (appears under Make once a recruiting cut is chosen): type one school per line, with a comma and the coach's name if you know it, for example Youngstown State, Coach Smith. Each school gets its own copy of the recruiting cut that opens on a card addressed to them. They take only a few seconds each to make and are listed with the other videos as "For Youngstown State".

13Renders & YouTube

Publish in the rail or the stage bar. Building takes you here by itself.

  • On the left: the build in progress, then every finished video with ▶ Play here, Open in video player and Show in File Explorer. There is also a DaVinci Resolve timeline (.fcpxml) next to each video: in Resolve use File → Import → Timeline.
  • On the right: Publish to YouTube. The first time, the box walks you through connecting your channel. After that: write one description, check the title of each video, tick the ones to upload, choose Private, Unlisted or Public, and press publish. They upload one after another.
Until Google has approved the app, YouTube keeps uploads Private whatever you choose. The box says "Uploaded as private" with a link to YouTube Studio, where you can change it. If the box asks you to connect again, the sign-in has run out (it lasts 7 days for now): press Connect.

14Players & the ScottForge card

  • Players (in the rail): name, number, position, class, school, height/weight, GPA, Hudl link, colours, logo. The accent colour is the ring colour.
  • ScottForge card (same page): the text of the animated ending and its colour. ▶ Play animation shows it.

15Good to know

  • Everything saves as you go; there is no save button.
  • A build takes a while for a long reel (each play is rendered from the 4K original). Don't close the app window while it builds.
  • If the page stops responding, close the Highlight Reels window and double-click the desktop shortcut again.
  • Reels for sharing on a phone: the finished video is in the project's reel folder.
  • Not sure whether it is still working? Open Activity in the rail.
