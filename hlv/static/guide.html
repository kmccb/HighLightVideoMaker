How to use Highlight Reels

From a folder of clips to a finished reel. The fast path is: New project → Mark plays → Build reel. The review page is there for fixing the snap and end of a play.

1Start the app

  • Double-click Highlight Reels on the desktop. It opens in your browser. If it is already running it just opens the page.
  • The app runs in a small minimised window called Highlight Reels. Closing that window stops the app.
  • The start screen lists your projects (one per player). Click one to open it.

2New project

  1. On the start screen click + New project.
  2. Pick the player (or quick-add a new one).
  3. Browse to the folder with that player's clips (photos in the same folder are picked up too).
  4. Choose the song: a music file, the player's last song, or paste a YouTube link.
  5. The app makes preview copies and finds the snap and end of every play. This takes a few minutes; you can watch the progress.
Clips from Google Drive: they are on your G: drive (Google Drive for desktop), so you can browse straight to G:\My Drive.

3Check each play (review page)

The app guesses where each play starts (the snap, green) and ends (red). The snap is usually close; the end is worth a quick look.

4Mark plays: the player and the hit

Open Mark plays ▸ from the review sidebar. One card per play; "only plays that still need it" hides the ones already done.

Player tab

Each card opens at the snap. Click your player (a magnifier follows the mouse). If he is hidden at the snap, step a little and click him there. Tracking him through the play starts by itself.

← →
a quarter second (Shift = one second)
S

click / drag

Backspace

Enter / ↓

The freeze and spotlight happen at the snap when you clicked him within 1.5 seconds of it. Otherwise the freeze happens where you first clicked him (that's the one moment we know for sure where he is).

Hit tab

The hit is the moment the reel lands on a strong beat (with the punch and slow-mo): contact or a tackle on defense, the catch or a big move on offense. Each card shows a filmstrip of the play; the dashed frame is the app's suggestion.

The suggestions learn from every hit you mark, for all players, and get better as you go. Tagging plays Offense or Defense lets each side learn on its own. The top of the Hit tab shows how often the suggestion is currently right.

The strip under the timeline shows tracking: green = following him, amber = unsure or far from a click (click him there), red = lost.

6Photos

  • Photos in the project folder (JPG, PNG, HEIC) are found automatically, also ones you add later.
  • They appear under the clips in the review sidebar and in the Build panel. Untick one to leave it out; click it to preview the slide.
  • They're placed by the date they were taken, next to the plays from the same day. Drag them anywhere in the Build panel.
  • Each photo is a slow pan-and-zoom slide over a blurred fill; Photo length (3/4/5 s) is fitted to the beat.
Phone screenshots look like a small picture inside a frame. You'll usually want to untick those.

7Build the reel

Click Build reel ▸ in the sidebar.

  • Format: Wide (16:9, TV/YouTube/Hudl), Vertical (9:16 for Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat: each play is re-cut from the original, following your player), or Both (two files; takes about twice as long).
  • Music: My song (hits synced to its beats) or Made for this reel: the app writes a Rock, Trap/rap or Cinematic track around your plays, with a big impact on every hit. ▶ Hear style plays a demo; New take gives a different key and chords. No copyright problems on Instagram/TikTok.
  • Intro: Slam, Inferno, Thunder, Shatter, Stadium, Glitch, Smoke, Neon, Warp, Gold, or Classic (still card). ▶ Preview intro shows it with this player's name and song.
  • Transition, Name banner (first play / every play / off), Freeze intro, Game audio level.
  • Sync hits to beat, Slow-mo into hit, Hit punch, Music starts at.
  • The list at the bottom is the reel order: drag to reorder, untick to leave out, Preview renders one play. Tags warn you about plays with no freeze or an unconfirmed trim.
  • ScottForge card adds the 7-second animated ending; Ending style picks Forge, Anvil strike, Molten or Minimal (one choice for all your reels).
  • Press Build reel. Plays already rendered are reused, so rebuilding after small changes is quicker.

When it's done: ▶ Play here, Open in video player or Show in File Explorer. There is also a DaVinci Resolve timeline (.fcpxml) next to the video: in Resolve use File → Import → Timeline.

8Players & the ScottForge card

  • Player profiles ▸: name, number, position, class, school, height/weight, colours, logo. The accent colour is the ring colour.
  • ScottForge card (same page): the text of the animated ending and its colour. ▶ Play animation shows it.

9Good to know

  • Everything saves as you go; there is no save button.
  • A build takes a while for a long reel (each play is rendered from the 4K original). Don't close the app window while it builds.
  • If the page stops responding, close the Highlight Reels window and double-click the desktop shortcut again.
  • Reels for sharing on a phone: the finished video is in the project's reel folder.
